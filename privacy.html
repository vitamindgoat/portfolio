Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This is the personal portfolio website of Oleksandr Rybitskyi. This page explains what information the site does and does not collect.

This site does not collect personal information. It does not use accounts, logins, or forms, and it does not run advertising or third-party tracking.

This site does not set cookies of its own. If the site is hosted on a service such as Vercel or GitHub Pages, that host may keep basic, anonymized server logs (for example, approximate region and browser type) for security and reliability. That data is handled by the host, not by this site.

Email

If you choose to email me using the address listed on this site, I will only use your message to reply to you. I will not add you to any list or share your email with anyone.

External links

This site links to other websites, such as GitHub. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and this policy does not cover them.
